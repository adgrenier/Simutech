# Glossary

**Logger**:
A physical data logger on the turbine that records channels at 50 Hz. There are four.

**Channel**:
One measured signal on a logger (a column in its raw file).

**Raw file**:
A CSV written by a logger holding 50 Hz samples of its channels, 10 minutes per file.

**Summary**:
A statistic of one channel computed over one whole UTC second, stamped at the start of that second.

**Backlog**:
All raw files that exist before the first periodic run.
